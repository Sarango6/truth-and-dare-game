📖 Rules & How to Play

👥 One Shared Device

Truth or Dare is designed for group gatherings using a single phone or tablet passed around the circle. No accounts or logins are needed.

🔒 Secret Submissions & Anonymity

During setup, each player secretly contributes 2 Truths and 2 Dares. Once all players submit, questions are shuffled anonymously into the game deck. Contributor identities are never displayed during gameplay.

❤️ 3 Lives Each

Every player starts with 3 lives. If you decline a challenge, you must sacrifice 1 life and perform a harmless penalty (like a 10-second silly dance!). Reaching 0 lives eliminates you from future turns.

🛡️ Safety Veto

Your comfort is paramount! If any challenge makes you uncomfortable, you can use the Safety Veto at any time. It has zero penalty, costs no lives, and the host will draw another challenge.
